Rules specific to `fr.po`.

## Subjunctive after "Il semble que"

After "Il semble que", always use the **subjunctive** mood, not the indicative.

- Correct: "Il semble qu'une demande … **ait été** reçue."
- Incorrect: "Il semble qu'une demande … **a été** reçue."

## Elision before vowel sounds

Apply French elision rules: "de" and "que" contract before words starting with a vowel or silent h.

- "de Internet Identity" → "**d'Internet** Identity"
- "que une" → "**qu'une**"

This applies to brand names and proper nouns too — "Internet" starts with a vowel, so "d'Internet" is correct.
