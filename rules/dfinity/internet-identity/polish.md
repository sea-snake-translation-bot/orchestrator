Rules specific to `pl.po`.

## Inclusive gender form for direct user-facing past tense

When a sentence addresses the user directly in the second-person past tense, use the inclusive `-łeś/aś` (or `-łam/em`) form rather than the masculine-only form. This matches the established convention throughout the catalog.

- Correct: `zalogowałeś/aś się`, `zapisałeś/aś`, `ustawiłeś/aś`, `usunąłeś/aś`
- Incorrect: `zalogowałeś się` (masculine-only)

Apply this to every new second-person past-tense verb added to `pl.po`.
